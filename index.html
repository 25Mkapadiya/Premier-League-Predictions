Model/Methodology

How the ensemble works

The goal is not to imitate a tip site. It is to combine independent signals, expose their contribution and test them with walk-forward validation.

01

Venue-adjusted attack & defense

Home and away scoring rates are estimated separately and shrunk toward league averages so noisy runs do not dominate.

02

Elo team strength

Opponent quality, score margin and home advantage contribute a slower-moving strength signal.

03

Promotion translation

Promoted teams are translated to Premier League scoring levels instead of treating Championship output as directly equivalent.

04

Poisson + Dixon-Coles score grid

Expected goals generate the full score distribution, with a low-score dependence correction around 0-0, 1-0, 0-1 and 1-1.

05

Opponent-adjusted recent form

The last eight finished matches are compared with what the structural model expected. Residual attack and defense performance is exponentially time-decayed and strongly shrunk toward neutral.

06

Bookmaker consensus, when connected

Decimal 1X2 prices are converted to vig-free probabilities bookmaker by bookmaker. Their median consensus receives a controlled blend weight rather than replacing the football model.

07

Pre-match context hook

Confirmed absences or lineup information can make small, sourced attack/defense adjustments. The default is exactly 1.00, so missing context contributes nothing.

Leakage policy

Every feature must exist before kickoff.

Recent form is computed only from matches completed before that fixture. Market snapshots and lineup/context adjustments must be timestamped before kickoff. Historical predictions should never be rewritten after the result.
