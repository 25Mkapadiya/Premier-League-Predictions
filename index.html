Model/Methodology

How the forecast works

A statistical football model with visible assumptions, historical validation and clear limitations.

01

Venue-adjusted attack & defense

Home and away scoring rates are estimated separately and shrunk toward league averages so short runs do not dominate the forecast.

02

Elo team strength

Three Premier League seasons contribute an Elo layer that accounts for opponent quality, score margin and home advantage.

03

Promotion translation

Coventry, Ipswich and Hull are translated from Championship performance to realistic promoted-team Premier League baselines rather than treated as equal-league data.

04

Poisson + Dixon-Coles

Expected goals generate a score matrix, with a low-score correction for the football-specific dependence around 0-0, 1-0, 0-1 and 1-1.
