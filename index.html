How the model works

No black-box “AI confidence” score. Every prediction comes from a reproducible statistical pipeline.

01

Venue-adjusted scoring

Home and away attack and defensive rates are estimated separately, then shrunk toward league averages to reduce noise.

02

Elo strength layer

Three Premier League seasons feed an Elo rating that responds to results, opponent quality, score margin, and home advantage.

03

Promotion translation

Coventry, Ipswich, and Hull are not treated as if Championship scoring transfers directly. Their rates are adjusted to historical promoted-team Premier League levels.

04

Dixon-Coles correction

A low-score correction improves Poisson estimates for 0-0, 1-0, 0-1, and 1-1 results, where football scores are not perfectly independent.
